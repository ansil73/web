Four steps

How it works

  1. Share your event details

    Send the date, place, number of guests and what you have in mind on WhatsApp.

  2. Plan the menu and counters

    We go through the menu, counters and services with you.

  3. We plan staff, counters and setup

    Service staff, hosts, décor, lights and equipment are arranged for the day.

  4. We serve, you celebrate

    Our team runs the counters and service so you can enjoy the event.
