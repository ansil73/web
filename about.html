Our story

About us

Since opening our first bakery in Kanjirappally, Kottayam, in 2005, Ulike has grown far beyond its original roots. What started as a small bakery has expanded into a well-rounded food service company. We’ve built on our reputation for quality with a range of services designed to meet diverse needs, from delicious bakery items to comprehensive event catering.

Today, Ulike encompasses several key areas: Ulike Caters and Events, which provides custom catering for various occasions; Ulike Distributors, handling the distribution of our top-notch products; and a full-service catering arm that supports other catering businesses. Our dedication to exceptional service and high standards continues to drive us, ensuring we deliver outstanding experiences for every customer and event.
